# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Two audiences, served equally: **customers** ordering real home-cooked food from verified local cooks (one-off orders, subscriptions/weekly boxes, bookable experiences), and **cooks ("preppers")** running a small food business through the app (menu, pricing, capacity, payouts, messaging with their customers). Both sides use the same app, on iOS, Android, or web, often on the go — a customer deciding what's for dinner, a cook checking orders between prep tasks.

## Product Purpose

Preppa is a real two-sided marketplace connecting home cooks with people who want home-cooked food nearby. It exists to make it easy and trustworthy to buy from — and sell as — a verified home kitchen: real payments (Stripe Connect), real payouts, real subscriptions, real messaging, real reviews. Success is both growth loops working together: customers ordering regularly (repeat orders, active subscriptions, booked experiences) and cooks getting verified, listed, and earning.

## Positioning

Real food from real local cooks — with the payment trust, polish, and reliability of a platform, not a listing board.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, appetite-forward, premium. Should make you want to eat, and make you trust the person cooking for you — hospitality and craft, not a gig-economy transaction. Apple-grade restraint in the chrome (one confident accent, calm neutrals, no clutter) so the warmth reads through photography, language, and a single well-used accent color rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Generic gig-delivery apps** (Uber Eats / DoorDash-style): no rainbow gradients, no cluttered promo banners, no coupon-code aesthetic.
- **Corporate SaaS / dashboard-cold** (Linear/Notion-style cold neutrals): this is a food app — it should feel inviting, not like enterprise software, even on the cook-facing hub/admin screens.
- **Cheap/DIY marketplace** (Craigslist/Facebook-Marketplace-shabby): trust and polish matter — real money and home-cooked food are involved on both sides.

## Design Principles

- **Trust is the product.** Verification, real payments, real payouts, real reviews — every design decision should reinforce "this is a real, accountable person," not a listing board.
- **Warmth without clutter.** Appetite and hospitality are carried by photography, color, and language — never by gimmicks, banners, or decoration.
- **Two-sided fairness.** Every surface serves either the customer ordering or the cook earning; neither experience is treated as secondary or bolted-on.
- **Calm confidence over hype.** One accent, restrained neutrals, no rainbow gradients or coupon-code visual noise — premium reads as restraint, not decoration.
- **One consistent language, everywhere.** The same design system across iOS, Android, and web — no per-platform reskinning, no "this feels like a different app on Android."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 text contrast (including placeholder text), reduced-motion support, standard touch targets. This is the working standard already in place in `theme.ts` — every fill/text color pair is contrast-verified before shipping, not eyeballed.
